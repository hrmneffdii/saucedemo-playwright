# BUG-001: "Add to cart" does not add certain products to the cart for `problem_user`

| Field | Details |
|---|---|
| **Severity** | High |
| **Priority** | P1 |
| **Status** | New |
| **Type** | Functional |
| **Component** | Inventory page / Shopping cart |
| **Reported by** | Herman Effendi |
| **Date reported** | 2026-10-28 |
| **Environment** | Brave Browser 156.1.98.60 , Ubuntu 24.04 , https://www.saucedemo.com |
| **Test account** | `problem_user` / `secret_sauce` |
| **Reproducibility** | 5 out of 5 attempts |

## Summary
After logging in as `problem_user`, clicking **Add to cart** on some products does not add them to the cart. The button label and the cart badge do not update, so the customer cannot purchase those products.

## Preconditions
- The cart is empty (use a fresh session or a new incognito window).
- The user is logged in as `problem_user`.

## Steps to Reproduce
1. Open https://www.saucedemo.com.
2. Enter Username `problem_user` and Password `secret_sauce`, then click **Login**.
3. On the inventory page, click **Add to cart** on each of the 6 products, one at a time.
4. After each click, observe the button label and the cart badge (top-right).
5. Click the cart icon and review the items listed.

## Expected Result
- After each click, the button changes to **Remove** and the cart badge increases by 1.
- After adding all 6 products, the badge shows **6** and the cart page lists all 6 products.

## Actual Result
- For some products, the button stays as **Add to cart** and the badge does not change.
- The cart badge shows fewer than 6 after clicking all 6 products, and the cart page lists fewer than 6 items.
- Affected products: Sauce Labs Bolt T-Shirt, Sauce Labs Fleece Jacket, and Test.allTheThings() T-Shirt (Red).
- No error message is shown to the user.

## Evidence
- Screen recording: `evidence/bug-001-add-to-cart.mp4`
- Screenshot of the badge after clicking all 6 products: `evidence/bug-001-badge.png`
- Control: the same steps as `standard_user` result in a badge of 6.
- Automated test: `tests/known-bugs.spec.ts`, "BUG-001: Add to cart button fails for some products" (marked with `test.fail()` while the bug exists).

## Impact
- **User:** the customer cannot buy the affected products and gets no feedback explaining why.
- **Business:** directly blocks the core purchase flow and causes lost sales.
- **Scope:** only `problem_user` was tested. Other accounts were not affected in my checks.

## Possible Cause (hypothesis, not confirmed)
The click handler for the affected products may not be wired to the cart state. This is a guess from black-box observation only, since the application source was not reviewed.

## Suggested Fix
Make the Add to cart handler update the cart state for every product and add a regression test that adds all 6 products and checks the badge value.

## Workaround
None found for the affected products in this session.

## Retest Criteria
- All 6 products can be added, the badge shows 6, and the cart page lists 6 items.
- The automated test for BUG-001 fails after the fix, which signals that `test.fail()` should be removed.
